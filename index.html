Ballistic Foley Lab

Sound computed from physics: projectiles, blasts, collapsing cavities, breaking structures, engines, rotors, electric arcs, energy weapons from launcher to target, forcefields, and your own recordings placed in the scene. The action filters then push the result toward a film mix.

Repeats the event on a timeline (bursts, salvos, swarms), or starts one at every hit of a recording. Each repeat is a fresh take. Loops ignore it.

Your sounds

Recordings and loaded files, kept in this browser until you remove them. Saved WAV files carry their date, input device, project, tags and notes (Broadcast WAV, iXML, XMP); Save all adds .xmp sidecars that Kurator, Lightroom and digiKam read as keywords.

Reference

Compare the Physics version with a recording of the real thing, then let one setting fit itself to it. Levels are left out: a recording's level is not known.
